Italiano

Come funziona lo studio e vivere sano

1. Doctolib e Segreteria

Prenota da solo su Doctolib: vai su prenota.savianu.it per visite e ricette abituali (evase in ~2 giorni lavorativi).

Segreteria (0575 910 904): chiama solo se non puoi usare Doctolib, per urgenze del giorno o visite domiciliari (09:30–10:30).

Certificato INPS: visita obbligatoria in studio il 1° giorno. Porta sempre le scatole dei farmaci.

2. Numeri Urgenze

CUP / Farmacia: 800 575 575 · Guardia Medica (notte/festivi): 116 117 · Emergenza grave: 112.

3. Farmaci e Stile di Vita

Pressione e diabete: prendi le compresse ogni giorno anche se stai bene. Antibiotici: solo con ricetta, finisci la scatola.

Salute: più verdure e legumi, meno sale/zucchero/riso bianco, 1,5–2 L d'acqua e 30 min di cammino al giorno.

Română

Cum funcționează cabinetul și viață sănătoasă

1. Doctolib și Secretariat

Programare pe Doctolib: intră pe prenota.savianu.it pentru consultații și rețete obișnuite (gata în ~2 zile lucrătoare).

Secretariat (0575 910 904): sună doar dacă nu poți folosi Doctolib, pentru urgențe de azi sau vizite acasă (09:30–10:30).

Certificat medical INPS: consultație obligatorie în prima zi. Adu mereu cutiile medicamentelor.

2. Numere de Urgență

CUP / Farmacie: 800 575 575 · Guardia Medica (noaptea/sărbători): 116 117 · Urgență gravă: 112.

3. Medicamente și Sănătate

Tensiune și diabet: ia tratamentul zilnic chiar dacă te simți bine. Antibiotice: doar cu rețetă, termină cutia.

Stil de viață: legume și leguminoase zilnic, puțină sare și zahăr, 1,5–2 L apă și 30 min mers pe jos zilnic.

English

How the clinic works & healthy living

1. Doctolib & Reception

Book on Doctolib: use prenota.savianu.it for appointments and repeat prescriptions (ready in ~2 working days).

Reception (0575 910 904): call only if you cannot use Doctolib, for same-day urgent issues or home visits (09:30–10:30).

Sick note (INPS): in-person visit mandatory on day 1. Always bring all your medicine boxes.

2. Emergency Numbers

CUP / Pharmacy: 800 575 575 · Out-of-hours doctor (24h): 116 117 · Life emergency: 112.

3. Medicines & Lifestyle

Blood pressure & diabetes: take pills daily even if feeling well. Antibiotics: prescription only, finish the pack.

Health: eat green vegetables and lentils, reduce salt/sugar/white rice, drink 1.5–2 L water, walk 30 min daily.

১. Doctolib ও সেক্রেটারি

Doctolib-এ বুকিং: অ্যাপয়েন্টমেন্ট ও নিয়মিত ওষুধের প্রেসক্রিপশনের জন্য prenota.savianu.it ব্যবহার করুন (~২ দিনে প্রস্তুত)।

সেক্রেটারি (0575 910 904): শুধুমাত্র Doctolib ব্যবহার না করতে পারলে বা আজকের জরুরি সমস্যায় কল করুন (হোম ভিজিট: 09:30–10:30)।

অসুস্থতার সার্টিফিকেট (INPS): প্রথম দিনেই চেম্বারে আসা বাধ্যতামূলক। সব ওষুধের বাক্স সাথে আনুন।

২. জরুরি নম্বরসমূহ

CUP / ফার্মেসি: 800 575 575 · Guardia Medica (রাত/ছুটির দিন): 116 117 · গুরুতর জরুরি অবস্থা: 112।

৩. ওষুধ ও স্বাস্থ্য

রক্তচাপ ও ডায়াবেটিস: সুস্থ বোধ করলেও প্রতিদিন ওষুধ খান। অ্যান্টিবায়োটিক: ডাক্তারের পরামর্শে পুরো কোর্স শেষ করুন।

খাবার ও ব্যায়াম: শাকসবজি ও ডাল বেশি খান, লবণ-চিনি-সাদা ভাত কমান, ১.৫–২ লিটার পানি পান করুন এবং প্রতিদিন ৩০ মিনিট হাঁটুন।
